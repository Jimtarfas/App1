Universal TV Remote: Zapply

Your iPhone becomes the remote for your Roku, Samsung or LG TV. No setup, just the same Wi-Fi.

  • Finds your TV on your Wi-Fi network automatically, or add it by IP address.
  • Full remote: direction pad, OK, back, home, volume, mute, playback and power.
  • App launcher: open the apps installed on your TV in one tap.
  • Premium: type with your phone's keyboard, change channels with a number pad, and control every TV in your home.

Works with Roku streaming players and Roku TVs, Samsung Smart TVs from 2016 onward (Tizen), and LG webOS Smart TVs.
