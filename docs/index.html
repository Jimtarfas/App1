Zapply: Big Button TV Remote

A TV remote that's easy to see and easy to use, for anyone who finds small buttons hard to read or press.

  • Big, labeled buttons in three sizes, so nobody has to guess what a symbol means.
  • High contrast and spoken button names, plus full VoiceOver support.
  • Voice control: ask Siri to turn the TV off, mute it, or turn it up or down.
  • One-tap routines, such as muting and switching off at bedtime.
  • Finds your TV on your Wi-Fi network automatically, and opens your TV's apps in one tap.
  • Premium: phone keyboard, channel buttons and number pad, every TV in your home, and unlimited routines.

Works with Roku streaming players and Roku TVs, Samsung Smart TVs from 2016 onward (Tizen), and LG webOS Smart TVs.
