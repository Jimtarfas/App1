Frequently asked questions

Which TVs does Zapply work with?

Roku streaming players and Roku TVs, Samsung Smart TVs from 2016 onward (Tizen), and LG Smart TVs running webOS.

Zapply can't find my TV

  • Make sure the TV is switched on and connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your iPhone. Guest networks, VPNs and some mesh setups keep devices apart.
  • Allow local network access: on your iPhone open Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and turn on Zapply.
  • In the app, open the TVs tab and tap Scan Again, or tap Add TV by IP Address and enter the address shown in your TV's network settings.

My Roku doesn't respond

On the Roku, open Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps and set Network access to Default.

My Samsung or LG TV won't connect

The first time you connect, your TV shows a prompt asking whether to allow the remote. Choose Allow (Samsung) or Yes (LG) with your TV's own remote. On Samsung, if you chose Deny earlier, open Settings › General › External Device Manager › Device Connection Manager › Device List and allow Zapply Remote. On some LG models you also need to turn on LG Connect Apps in the network settings.

Can Zapply switch my TV on?

Roku TVs with Fast TV Start can be switched on from Zapply. Samsung and LG TVs don't accept power-on commands from apps over Wi-Fi, so switch them on with the TV's own remote. Zapply can switch all supported TVs off.

How do I make the buttons bigger or easier to see?

Open the Settings tab. Under Remote you can choose the Big Buttons or Full Remote layout, pick Standard, Large or Extra Large buttons, and turn on High Contrast or Speak Button Names.

How do I control the TV with Siri?

Connect to your TV in Zapply once. Then say, for example, "Turn off the TV with Zapply", "Mute the TV with Zapply" or "Turn the TV up with Zapply". You can also say "Run Bedtime in Zapply" to run a routine. All of these actions are also in the Shortcuts app. Voice commands use the TV you connected to last.

What is a routine?

A routine presses several buttons for you in one tap. For example, the Bedtime routine mutes the TV, waits a second and switches it off. Open the Routines tab to run, edit or create routines. Free accounts can keep up to 3 routines.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks typing with your iPhone keyboard, the channel buttons and number pad, saving and switching between more than one TV, and unlimited routines. The Big Buttons and Full Remote layouts, volume, playback, the app launcher, Siri voice commands and up to 3 routines are free.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Zapply and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I bought Premium but it isn't unlocked

Open the app's Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Can I try Zapply without a TV?

Yes. In the TVs tab, tap Try the Demo TV to see a virtual TV that responds to every button.
