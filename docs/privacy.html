Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Universal TV Remote: Zapply ("Zapply", "the app") lets you control your TV from your iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

Information we collect

None. Zapply has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We don't receive your name, contacts, location, usage data or device identifiers.

Your local network and your TV

To find and control your TV, Zapply asks iOS for local network access. With it, the app sends requests only to devices on the Wi-Fi network your iPhone is connected to, to find Roku, Samsung and LG TVs and to send them the buttons you press, the text you type and the apps you open. This traffic goes directly between your iPhone and your TV and never passes through our servers. We don't run any servers for the app.

The app stores a few things on your iPhone so it can reconnect quickly: the names and local addresses of the TVs you've used, and the pairing codes your Samsung or LG TV issues when you approve the connection. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app. You can also remove a TV in the app's TVs tab.

Text you type with the in-app keyboard is sent only to your TV and is not stored by the app.

Siri, Shortcuts and spoken button names

When you use Siri or the Shortcuts app with Zapply, Apple handles your voice request and tells the app only which action to run, such as "turn off the TV". "Speak Button Names" uses the speech voices built into iOS on your device. Zapply doesn't record or send any audio.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

Children

Zapply does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
