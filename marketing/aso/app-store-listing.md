## Description
Zapply is a TV remote designed to be easy to see and easy to use. Every key is large and says what it does in words, so nobody has to guess what a tiny symbol means. It works over your Wi-Fi with Roku players and TVs, Samsung Smart TVs (2016 and later) and LG webOS TVs.

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
• Big Buttons layout: the controls people use most, each with an icon and a word label
• Three button sizes: Standard, Large and Extra Large
• High contrast mode: black keys with heavy white outlines and a bright yellow OK button
• Speak Button Names: each press is read aloud (it stays quiet while VoiceOver is on)
• Full VoiceOver labels and haptic feedback on every press
• Prefer every button? Switch to the Full Remote layout at any time

CONTROL YOUR TV WITH YOUR VOICE
• "Turn off the TV with Zapply", "Mute the TV with Zapply", "Turn the TV up with Zapply"
• Run your routines by name, for example "Run Bedtime in Zapply"
• Use the actions in the Shortcuts app and its automations

ONE-TAP ROUTINES
• A routine presses several buttons for you, for example mute, wait, then switch off at bedtime
• Build routines from button presses, opening an app, typing text and short waits
• Free accounts keep up to 3 routines

ALSO INCLUDED
• Finds your TV on your Wi-Fi network automatically, or add it by IP address
• Direction pad, OK, back, home, volume, mute, playback and power
• App launcher for the apps installed on your TV
• A Demo TV that shows a virtual screen, so you can try every button before you connect

ZAPPLY PREMIUM
• Type on your TV with your iPhone keyboard
• Channel buttons and a number pad
• Save and switch between every TV in your home
• Unlimited routines

## Review notes
WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST REVIEW (guidelines 4.3 and 4.2.6)
We rebuilt the app around one audience: people who find small remotes and small, icon-only remote apps hard to use, such as older adults and people with low vision or limited dexterity. New in this build:
- Big Buttons layout (now the default): fewer controls, each large and labeled with a word, in three sizes.
- High contrast mode and Speak Button Names (spoken feedback that stays quiet when VoiceOver is on).
- Siri and Shortcuts: seven App Intents (turn off, mute, volume up/down, press any button, open a TV app, run a routine) with spoken phrases.
- Routines: user-built sequences of button presses, app launches, waits and typed text, runnable in one tap or by voice.
- A brand-first name, no subscription screen at launch, and a single yearly plan instead of weekly and yearly.

No account or sign-in is needed.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT A TV
1. Launch the app, tap Continue, choose a button size, and tap Find My TV. Allow local network access when iOS asks.
2. In the TVs tab, tap "Try the Demo TV". A virtual TV appears on the Remote tab and responds to every button.
3. In Settings > Remote, try Button Size, High Contrast and Speak Button Names, or switch to the Full Remote layout.
4. In the Routines tab, tap Run next to Bedtime: the Demo TV mutes and switches off. Tap New Routine to build one.
5. Siri: after the Demo TV (or a real TV) has been connected once, say "Turn off the TV with Zapply". The actions also appear in the Shortcuts app under Zapply.
