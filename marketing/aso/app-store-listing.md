## Review notes
Zapply is a remote control for Roku, Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) TVs on the same Wi-Fi network. It finds TVs by checking addresses on the local subnet, which needs the Local Network permission. No account or sign-in is needed.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT A TV
1. Launch the app and tap Find My TV. You can close the subscription screen with the X in the top-left corner.
2. Allow local network access when iOS asks.
3. In the TVs tab, tap "Try the Demo TV". A virtual TV appears on the Remote tab and responds to every button: direction pad, OK, back, home, menu, volume, mute, play/pause, rewind, fast forward and power. The Apps tab lists the demo TV's apps and opens them on the virtual screen.

PREMIUM (auto-renewable subscriptions in the "Zapply Premium" group)
- com.zapplyremote.app.premium.weekly: $4.99 per week
- com.zapplyremote.app.premium.yearly: $29.99 per year, with a 3-day free trial
Premium unlocks the phone keyboard, the channel buttons and number pad, and saving more than one TV. Locked controls show a gold padlock, and tapping one opens the subscription screen. Restore Purchases is on the subscription screen and in Settings. The Terms of Use (Apple's standard EULA) and the Privacy Policy are linked on the subscription screen, in Settings and in the App Store description.

Zapply is an independent app and isn't affiliated with Roku, Samsung or LG. It uses the local network control interfaces these TVs provide for remote control apps (Roku External Control Protocol on port 8060, Samsung on ports 8001/8002, LG webOS on ports 3000/3001).
